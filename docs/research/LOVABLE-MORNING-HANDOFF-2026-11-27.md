## Highest-value review targets

1. **Real reader functionality.** Session 2 recorded 250 native observations,
   but zero field values or button-invocation events. Synthetic tests alone had
   not established compatibility with the mixed WPF/DevExpress/Win32 MDI app.
   The v0.1.16 fix collapses same-role nested wrapper/editor aliases, preserves
   independent sibling ambiguity, bounds outer-scope discovery and prunes dense
   grids before the field walk. Evaluate remaining provider/lineage/coverage gaps
   and concrete read-only entry points supported by installer evidence.
2. **Events.** UIA listener readiness does not prove Invoke events propagate
   across provider boundaries. Explain the zero-event failure and propose a
   practical bounded test/fix; a screen change is not a verified save. Done is
   now a recognized static button-intent label. No blanket desktop harvesting.
3. **Dataset and feedback.** Field observations, incoming source, generated
   suggestion, copied/transferred draft and accepted final output retain distinct
   provenance. Audit optional ratings, deferred explanations, retries, history
   merging, browser persistence, and replay without duplicates or stale joins.
   A ten/unrated translation creates no new discrepancy; unrated is unknown.
   Session 2 also shows a rating on card one with a comment about card two;
   assess whether rating scope is clear and group evidence remains available.
4. **SIG functionality.** Focus on sliding scales, split character budgets,
   treatment clauses/shift schedules and preservation of qualifiers. The narrow
   `PRN. IF` punctuation correction is now covered by a synthetic case. Avoid a
   wholesale regex rewrite without a demonstrated functional benefit.
5. **Batch-aware drafts.** The final Paxit sweeper target is invariant across
   midnight: dispensed/start/delivery dates match the sweeper batch date, quantity
   covers the full ordered three days, days supply is 3, delivery ID starts with
   9. Midnight changes defaults needing correction, not the desired target.
   Do not turn three days into three doses or hardcode one demonstrated route.

Trust the technician. Prefer useful drafts, visible uncertainty and optional
comments over new processing barriers. FWDirect remains excluded. Keep the
temporary intensified logging HIGH-removal markers visible.
