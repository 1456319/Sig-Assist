TESTS MUST PASS; DO NOT CHEAT WITH HARDCODING RESPONSES TO EXACT INPUTS. MINOR DEVIATIONS IN OPTIMAL OUTPUTS MAY BE ACCEPTABLE (EX: 'WITH FOOD' VS 'WITH MEALS'; THESE ARE CONSIDERED INTERCHANGABLE). FOR EXAMPLE, A SIG FOR TRULICITY 4.5MG/0.5ML SHOULD PRODUCE THE CORRECT OPTIMAL OUTPUT, EVEN THOUGH IT ISN'T A DRUG SPECIFICALLY LISTED IN THE TEST. THE PROGRAM SHOULD IDENTIFY THAT THERE IS A TARGET DOSE BASED ON THE USER INPUT, AND CALCULATE THE CORRECT VOLUME TO INJECT AND THE DOSE THAT THIS WILL DELIVER. AVOID LARGE IF/THEN BLOCKS WHICH WILL DISRUPT REAL-WORLD USE CASES.

HARDCODED SIGS MAY BE PRESENT, BUT ONLY AS A TEMPLATE FOR A SPECIFIC DRUG THAT MUST BE COMBINED WITH THE SUER INPUT TO PRODUCE AN OPTIMAL OUTPUT. FOR EXAMPLE, "POLYETH GLYC PWD PACKET (17GM)

OPTIMAL OUTPUT: MIX 17 GM (1 PACKET) IN 8OZ OF WATER AND GIVE PO QD FCON"
